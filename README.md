# Akan Name Generator

A simple web app that calculates the day of the week you were born on and
gives you the traditional Akan (Ghanaian) name for that day.

## How it works

1. Enter your birth day, month, and year.
2. Select your gender.
3. Click **Find My Akan Name**.
4. The app calculates the day of the week you were born and shows the
   matching Akan name.

## Akan Day Names
 

Sunday     Kwasi      Akosua      
 Monday    Kwadwo     Adwoa        
 Tuesday    Kwabena    Abenaa      
 Wednesday  Kwaku     Akua        
 Thursday  Yaw        Yaa         
 Friday    Kofi      Afua        
Saturday   Kwame      Ama
